## Lessons this stretch

- **Diagnosing first paid twice.** The no-link instrument settled "one fix or two" (one), and it found finding 118, which was not the engine's and would have voided a v19 round if it had surfaced after the link.
- **An instrument at layer N cannot say what layer N-1 did.** "Nothing reached the hook" became "Writer emits nothing" in the write-up; the 10c de-duplication story was never tested either way.
- **A positive control and a NOT MEASURED state kept a wrong red case from being read.** v8 and v18 gave identical numbers on an HTML 404 page; the runner said NOT MEASURED, not RED.
- **A broken log idiom ran through four scripts and a handoff sentence** before a traceback logged `exit=0` and exposed it. A check that cannot print anything but success is not a check.
- **`pass: true` is only as old as the runner.** The page fix made M2-4's runner verdict true while the criterion, since §31.2, is still red.
- **Red before green, in that order, stayed cheap.** 118's fix was written after P1/P2 had run, and a second pair of runs (P3/P4) was the whole verification.
- **A prediction that missed (D2) cost nothing** because it was written as a prediction and used only to confirm a mechanism the fix does not rely on.
