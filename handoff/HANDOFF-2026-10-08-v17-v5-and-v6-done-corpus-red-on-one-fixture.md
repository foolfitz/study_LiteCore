## Lessons this stretch

- **An arm's design limits what it can see.** The full-TTC control arm found something no subset-versus-subset dry run could: the dry run's three arms were all subsets, so whatever differs between the subset and the full TTC was invisible to it. The red appears only because the control arm was a different pack.
- **A pre-registered "first suspect" was wrong, and the miss was recorded.** The corpus prediction named Korean punctuation advances; the first differing line is pure ASCII. The prediction's own instruction ("look first at which characters sit on its first differing line") is what exposed it; it was followed, not argued with.
- **An independent reviewer caught two factual errors in my own upstream draft that my self-review had missed** (the local tree also modifies `vcl/qt5/QtFrame.cxx`; `fontconfig.cxx:1420` is in `matchFont`, not `Substitute`), and did a native single-variable reproduction I had not. Review is worth most where the author's reading of the source is the only evidence.
- **A discriminator whose variants may not have applied cannot refute.** H-mono's three variants gave identical bands; "the slot is not the cause" holds only if the automatic style applied, which was not checked, and the Western slot was never varied. The failure narrowed less than it looked.
- **A positive control can hit while the arm still carries an extra variable.** The E02 discriminator's C2 hit; its C1 missed because native loads LO's own `fc_local.conf` and OpenSymbol. The reading was written with that caveat instead of "H-wasm" alone.
