== THE CONTROL ==

'make -n over 18 targets is byte-identical before and after' does NOT hold
this round, and pretending otherwise would be the wrong claim.  Makefile is
a prerequisite of build/r5/writer-review/exports.txt and of every %.o rule,
and the owner's 2026-09-09 v13 link regenerated exports.txt at 12:10:37 --
NEWER than the Makefile's 2026-09-08 07:49 mtime.  So any edit at all, or a
bare touch, makes those text files stale again.  The v13 round got
byte-identity because that file was already older than the Makefile.

The property that does hold is the one that matters, and it is measured by
replacing the edit with a touch:

  cp Makefile.before Makefile   # original CONTENT, fresh mtime
  make -n over the 18 targets   -> sha256 c054bc1c4b6c9278124621740e2e2a6bdbb39c2ecee73c260755435dd6fa3cf3
  cp Makefile.after Makefile    # the v14 block
  make -n over the 18 targets   -> sha256 c054bc1c4b6c9278124621740e2e2a6bdbb39c2ecee73c260755435dd6fa3cf3

BYTE-IDENTICAL.  The v14 block adds exactly zero work to any existing
target; the whole delta against the baseline is the mtime.

Baseline for reference: sha256 cfef4b019b41228ecd3ff8528661372a0c8ede6406b970bda409cfe823bbd8d6

== WHAT THE DELTA IS MADE OF ==

       22 continuation line of the above
        8 em++ compile or link
      388 exports.txt regeneration (text files)

The four extra em++ on e2-editor-v13 are its remaining product .o files;
its target was ALREADY at 2 em++ in the baseline, because
src/probe_engine.cpp went in at 20:21 on 2026-09-09 and v13's objects are
from 12:10.  That is the overwrite hazard the v14 block exists to remove,
and it predates this edit.
