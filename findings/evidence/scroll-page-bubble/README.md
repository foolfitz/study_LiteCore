The owner's request of 2026-10-14 (「拉著卷軸的游標旁邊會有一個泡泡顯示當前頁數」), ruled the same day to show on ANY scroll of the desk and to fade after scrolling stops, accepted as specified (「OK 照規格走」). ## The no-host half (N1–N6, D1, K1, Z1) — `n-nohost-v20/`

**What ran.** The `dist/` mirror after `make e2-c-assets` — page `ccb67a52a3fe4b73…`, session `f47c80ec…`, profile `e2-editor-v20` — copied by `run_e2_c_product_path.build_mirror` and served by `web/serve.py` on a free port; the 60-page fixture (`create_long_document.build(60)`, served as `probe-bubble-long60.odt`) and `dist/blank.odt`; headless Firefox 157.0.1 through WebDriver and headless Chrome 151.0.7922.169 through CDP, both with real input (WebDriver actions / `Input.dispatch*`), 1850 wide. Never `--write`; every report records the served page's sha256 at its start and its end (all `ccb67a52…`), and the judge refuses a report that served anything else (`--page ccb67a52`). Each arm's preflight is in its record: KWallet `b false`, `pgrep -c chrome` 33–38 (the owner's own Chrome; our Chrome is `--headless=new`), nothing of ours running.

**The instrument** — `probe_bubble.py`, built on 10-14n's `probe_10_14o.py` (`findings/evidence/host-0.2.21/`): the same `Worker.postMessage` hook and the same state log, with the bubble (`data-shown`, text, rect, computed opacity / transition / `checkVisibility()`, the aria attributes) and `#a11y-para` added to every state entry, a `sc` log entry per `scroll` event of `#desk`, and a screenshot per wheel step. Arms: `bubble-wheel` (the 10-14n page-number steps: 14 × 700, 3 × 3000, back to top; a read and a screenshot 0.6 s after each), `bubble-reduce` (3 steps under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` — Chrome `Emulation.setEmulatedMedia`, Firefox pref `ui.prefersReducedMotion=1`), `bubble-drag` (press on the thumb the screenshot shows, 10 × +10 px at 50 ms, release), `bubble-keys` (click the first line, Home, ArrowDown × 60 at 60 ms), `bubble-short` (`--doc blank`: the window made tall enough that nothing scrolls, three wheels, then back to the normal height and one wheel as the positive control). The thumb reader is `bubble_geometry.py` (the spec's method: the column `x ∈ [desk.right − 12, desk.right − 1]`, ink = luminance more than 40 from the column's modal colour, the longest ink run of at least 12 rows; `None` when there is none). The judge is `judge_bubble.py`; its output for this run is `n-nohost-v20/judge.txt`.

**Firefox N2 was instrument-red, and why** (`firefox-headless-bar-experiment/`). The Firefox wheel arm read the thumb on 0 of 17 steps: in every screenshot the desk's bar column (`x 1838–1849`; the bar is 12 px — `offsetWidth 1850 − clientWidth 1838`) is pure white in all 716 rows, at rest, at the top and before the drag. A static page with the product's desk geometry (716 high, scrollHeight 74182, a 12 px bar) in the same headless Firefox shows the cause: the default (GTK-themed) bar paints **no thumb at all** in a WebDriver screenshot — at rest, with the pointer on the rail, and 0.2 s after a real wheel; `scrollbar-width: thin` narrows the bar to 6 px and still paints nothing; only `scrollbar-color` makes Firefox paint a thumb (6 px wide, 34 px long, read at 272.0 for scrollTop 5600, against the product model's 277.5). `result.txt` has the five readings; the five screenshots are beside it. A `bubble-wheel-hover` arm the first draft of the probe carried, on the guess that the thumb paints under the pointer, was removed after this — hovering paints nothing.

The host colours its bars by CSS: in K4's screenshots the thumb is rgb(125, 125, 125) in **both** browsers, 6 px wide inside the 12 px bar, and the track shows the desk's background — so the spec's §2 inference that Firefox's bar is an overlay bar of zero width was wrong (the host's Firefox bar is a 12 px CSS-coloured bar; this is appended to the spec). The supplement therefore sets what the host sets: `probe_bubble.py --css-bar '#7d7d7d transparent'` (Firefox only) puts `scrollbar-color` on `#desk` right after the instrument is installed and records it in the report (`cssBar`: computed `rgb(125, 125, 125) rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)`, `offsetWidth − clientWidth` 12 before and 12 after). The product places the bubble from `offsetWidth − clientWidth` alone, which this does not touch, so the bubble under the supplement is placed exactly as without it; what changes is only that the camera can see the thumb. The supplement ran the wheel and drag arms into `firefox-css-bar/` (23:48:34–23:49:37); the original Firefox run is untouched and its N2 stays recorded as INSTRUMENT_RED and its D1 as NOT_ESTABLISHED (the instrument had no thumb to press on — a different case from the spec's "the drag moved nothing"). The judge's rule across runs: FAIL beats PASS beats NOT_ESTABLISHED beats INSTRUMENT_RED, per browser and criterion, and a criterion no run covers is MISSING.
