# Finding 093, option B, step 1 — acceptance, registered before the probe exists

Written 2026-09-08, **before a single line of the probe was written and before
any number was measured**. Nothing below may be moved afterwards; a correction
is appended and names what it replaces (AGENTS.md §10).

`handoff/RUNBOOK-operator-093-engine-geometry.md` §2 — the coordinate space —
is **read from source, not measured**. Three things are unknown: the unit
`XAccessibleComponent::getBounds()` returns, the origin it is relative to, and
whether converting it to twips lines up with the caret rectangle LOK already
gives the page. Step 1 settles those on the **native** build
`build-native-26-8`, which is rebuildable, mints no wasm identity, and voids no
evidence.

**This document decides nothing about option B's shape.** It says what "lines
up" means numerically, so that the answer is read off a measurement rather than
argued.

---

## 0. The stop rule this acceptance serves

The runbook's step 1 says: **if the correspondence answer is "does not line
up", stop and report; do not proceed to any source change.** That makes the
tolerance below load-bearing in one specific direction — a tolerance chosen
after seeing the numbers could turn a stop into a go. Hence it is registered
here, with its justification, before the probe exists.

## 2. Q1 — the unit

Answered by a measured ratio, not by a convention.

Let `r = rawHeight / twipHeight`, computed over the full span from the first
probed paragraph's top to the last probed paragraph's bottom (a span of tens of
thousands of twips, so integer quantisation cannot decide it).

| measured `r` | verdict |
| --- | --- |
| `r` within 2% of `dpi / 1440` | **output-device pixels** (the runbook's reading) |
| `r` within 2% of `1.0` | twips |
| `r` within 2% of `2540 / 1440 = 1.7639` | 1/100 mm |
| anything else | **none of the three** — reported as an unknown unit, not rounded to the nearest hypothesis |

`dpi` is not assumed. It is read three independent ways and all three are
reported:

1. `OutputDevice::GetDPIX()` / `GetDPIY()`;
2. `LogicToPixel(Size(1440, 1440), MapMode(MapUnit::MapTwip))` — pixels per inch
   by construction;
3. the device's own `GetMapMode()` (unit, origin, and both scale fractions).

**If those disagree, the disagreement is the answer** and no single DPI is
named. The runbook's §2.1 already records that "that DPI in this wasm build has
never been measured by anybody"; naming an unmeasured 96 here would be exactly
the error the runbook flags.

## 3. Q2 — the origin

The runbook §2.2 *argues* document-absolute, from `getBoundsImpl(true)` skipping
the parent subtraction when `pParent->IsRootFrame()`
(`sw/source/core/access/acccontext.cxx:810-815`) plus a flat tree. Step 1 does
not inherit the argument. Three things are measured, and **all three** must hold
for the verdict "document-absolute":

- **O-1** — the accessible parent of every probed paragraph is, by UNO object
  identity, the same context the walk started from (`getAccessibleParent()`'s
  context `==` the walk root). Reported per row, not asserted once.
- **O-2** — the converted-to-twips `y` is **strictly increasing** over the
  probed paragraphs taken in document order, and the last probed paragraph's
  `y` exceeds the output window's height in twips by at least 2×. A
  view-relative or parent-relative origin cannot produce that: it would clamp or
  wrap.
- **O-3** — `getLocationOnScreen() − getBounds().topLeft` is the **same
  constant** (both components) for every probed paragraph, to within 1 device
  pixel. A per-paragraph-varying difference would mean the two are anchored
  differently and the "absolute" reading is wrong.

If O-1..O-3 hold but §4 fails, the origin is reported as *self-consistent but
not the document origin*, and the offset is reported in twips.

## 4. Q3 — the correspondence, and the tolerance

For each probed paragraph `N`:

- `paraTop`, `paraBottom` — the converted-to-twips band of `getBounds()`;
- `caretTop`, `caretH` — the LOK caret rectangle's `y` and `height`, in twips;
  `caretBottom = caretTop + caretH`.

`caretH` is the height LOK reports for the line the caret is on, so expressing
the tolerance as a fraction of it makes it *a fraction of a line* by
construction rather than by assumption. The absolute value in twips is reported
on every row so the number is legible without recomputing it.

### The two gating conditions

- **C1 — containment.** `caretTop >= paraTop − T_contain` **and**
  `caretBottom <= paraBottom + T_contain`, with **`T_contain = 0.5 × caretH`**
  (half a line at each edge).
- **C2 — top alignment.** `|caretTop − paraTop| <= T_align`, with
  **`T_align = 2 × caretH`** (two lines). The caret is on the paragraph's
  **first** line by construction: the search marker `LD-Pnn-Lmm` is the first
  token of the paragraph.

**Why two conditions and not one.** C1 alone is satisfied by a bounds rectangle
that spans the whole document — containment is necessary, not sufficient. C2 is
what makes the pair discriminating.

**Why `T_align = 2 × caretH` and not tighter.** The tolerance is chosen from
what the fix has to buy, not from what the numbers turn out to be. O-FIX
(`findings/evidence/093/ORACLE-fix-shape.md`) needs paragraph N's band to
overlap a viewport ~39 lines tall by at least half a line; an error of two lines
cannot cost that overlap, and an error of two lines is also far below the
spacing of the red case in §5. A tighter figure would risk a false **stop** on
what the runbook itself predicts is a legitimate frame-area difference
(paragraph spacing), and the stop rule is not reversible by re-reading. A looser
figure would stop discriminating.

**Registered in advance, because it is the tempting exit:** if C2 fails with a
`dTop` that is *constant across paragraphs* to within `T_contain`, that is
reported as `constant-bias` **and it is still a FAIL** of this acceptance. The
runbook's stop rule then applies. It is the owner's call, not the drafter's,
whether a constant bias is a redesign or a subtraction.

### The verdict

`lines-up` iff **every** probed row passes **both** C1 and C2, over a probed set
of **at least 8 paragraphs**, spread across the document, **including at least
one whose `paraTop` exceeds the output window height** (so O-2's alternative is
actually exercised rather than merely unreached).

Anything else is `does-not-line-up`, and the runbook's stop rule fires: report,
change no source.

## 5. The red case — required, and required to go red

A probe that cannot print a different value is not a probe (AGENTS.md §6).

**R-1 — shifted pairing.** Every row is evaluated a second time against the
caret rectangle of a **different** paragraph, one at least **10 probed
positions away** in document order. With 18 paragraphs to an A4 page, ten
positions is more than half a page — an order of magnitude past `T_align`.

**Required outcome:** every shifted row **FAILS** C1 and C2. If any shifted row
passes, the agreement reported in §4 is structural rather than discriminating,
and the whole measurement is void — reported as such, not repaired.

**R-2 — non-constancy.** The converted `paraTop` must differ between probed
rows (a probe returning one constant for every paragraph would satisfy nothing
here but must still be caught explicitly).

Both red cases are computed by the judge from the same raw capture as the green
one, so they cannot silently be run against different bytes.

## 6. What step 1 does **not** establish

- **Nothing about the wasm artifact.** Native and wasm are different builds and
  finding 036 is about exactly that. This measures the *coordinate space of the
  API*, which is core's, not the artifact's.
- **Nothing about cost.** The runbook §7.3 asks what 133 `getBounds()` calls per
  state read cost. That is a separate measurement and is not attempted here.
- **Nothing about the projection.** Whether a screen reader then scrolls the
  node into view is finding 069's precedent and is still unmeasured (phase 3's
  named limitation).
