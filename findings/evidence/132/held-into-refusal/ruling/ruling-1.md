## WHY（給擁有者，白話）

裁決：**A＝B＋D 現在做（只改頁面、不用連結），C 排進連結批次。**

1. 量到的事：在文件結尾按住 Delete，被拒一次、自動重開之後，游標跑到文件**開頭**，按住的 Delete 就從開頭往後刪——Chrome 刪掉 82／83 字、Firefox 71／72 字、各併掉一段——而且**存回伺服器了**。這不是「限制」，是改了文件的缺陷，登錄成 finding 134。
2. 它不是 132 弄出來的：自動重開是 109 的修法（10-06）帶進來的，重開後游標本來就永遠在開頭；132 之前的頁面一樣。
3. 只做 B（被拒之後、同一次按住的重複全部丟掉）擋得住「按住」，但單按一次 Delete 再打字，字還是落到開頭；只做 D（重開後把游標放回原位）擋得住單按，但按住會一路拒到第三次、要重新整理頁面。所以兩個都要。
4. C（引擎在兩端什麼都不做、不拒絕）才是根治，但要連結；照你的規矩，不用連結的先出、引擎改動攢一次。C 來了之後 B＋D 也不是白做：表格的拒絕、119 那種重開，C 管不到。
5. F2（畫面有沒有跟著跳到開頭）不用先量：量了也不改處方；讀碼是「會跳、而且跟著刪」。§13.1 那句「看不到」現在就補更正，下一次探針順便記 scrollTop。
6. 驗收：探針加一臂「結尾單按 Delete、重開後打字」；每一臂逐輪判——拒絕恰好 1 次、世代 1→2、伺服器檔案與原檔相同、標記落在最後一段。先紅一次：run-1 本身就是 B 的紅案；D 的紅案要在 0.2.15 上把新臂跑一次（預期標記落在第 0 段）。
7. 出貨是宿主 0.2.16、殼層 v65，仍是未計數候選；完整計數照 §9.21 併到連結批次。沒跑驗收就等於沒修：0.2.15 還是候選、134 開著。

## CONDITIONS

1. **B — page (`e2-editor-app.js`), the hold stops at the first refusal.** After a delete action this page sent (Backspace/Delete, in any form `deleteActionFor` chose) is rejected by the session, no further Backspace/Delete `keydown` with `repeat === true` reaches the engine until that hold ends. A hold ends on a `keyup` of Backspace/Delete observed at **window** level in the capture phase (the sink is disabled during the reopen and events land on `body` — run-1 `keydownsElsewhere: ['body-user']`), on a `keydown` of that key with `repeat === false`, or on window `blur`. Presses always go out (finding 131). Dependency carried from H2, to be named in the spec: the `repeat` flag is the driver's; a keyboard path without it gets today's behaviour, no new harm. Unit criterion U-B in `editor-shell-v2/tests/page-delete-and-sink.test.mjs`, same sliced-source pattern as 132 U3/U4: refused-hold set → repeat dropped at depth 0; press at depth 0 not dropped; after the hold ends → repeat at depth 0 not dropped; the three "other keys" still not dropped. **Red first:** the new assertions run against the page as it is (`abe93f45…`) must fail by name.
2. **D — page, the caret goes back after a reopen.** In `reopenAfterBoundaryRefusal`: read the engine-reported caret (`session.state.snapshot.editorState.caret`, finite `x`, `y`, `height`) **before** `session.restart()`; after `restart()` returns keep `el.sink.focus({ preventScroll: true })` first (the B3x lesson at `:1676-1683` stands), then `session.placeCaret(x, y + height / 2)`. Fail-soft: no finite caret, or placeCaret rejects → the session stays exactly as `restart()` left it and the post-reopen toast says the caret is at the document start (one sentence; no claim it was restored). Applies to every path through `reopenAfterBoundaryRefusal` — boundary and 119's `deleteUnverified` — not only the two ends.
