## Metrics that the frozen bridge cannot establish

v23-poll has no engine timestamp. Page event-receipt differences are browser delivery intervals, not engine tick intervals. The worker projects raw LOK id0 (INVALIDATE_TILES) and id1 (INVALIDATE_VISIBLE_CURSOR) into the same document-invalidated event without preserving the id; combined invalidation receipt times cannot prove first-tile latency. Geometry remaining unchanged during the fixed windows is not proof that background engine layout completed. These three exact measurements remain explicitly NE. Raw events, request/reply timestamps, step durations, keyToUndoMs when present, geometry samples and errors remain in each report for direct inspection. No extra core instrumentation is smuggled into the040 successor for these record-only metrics.
